Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 22, 2026

Last Updated: June 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Deghost edu mobile application (collectively, the "Application" or "Service"), operated by DeGhost ("we", "us", "our", or the "Service Provider").

We are committed to protecting the privacy of school administrators, parents, guardians, and students. This Privacy Policy explains how data is accessed, authenticated, and secured within the Application, specifically tailored to comply with international data protection frameworks and mobile app marketplace requirements, including the Apple App Store Review Guidelines.

Important Core Principle: Deghost edu operates as a secure portal/client interface for participating educational institutions ("Schools"). The Application does not request, solicit, or collect new or additional personal registration data from parents or students. Authentication and data display rely entirely on pre-existing records safely managed and authorized by your child’s school.
---

1. Data Architecture & Authentication Model

To ensure maximum data minimization and security, the Application functions under the following data structure:

  • Zero Self-Registration: Parents and legal guardians cannot open or create independent accounts within the Application.
  • Pre-Verified School Authentication: Access is granted strictly via the mobile phone number or credentials already officially registered, verified, and maintained in the respective School's database.
  • Real-Time Data Verification: When a parent attempts to log in, the Application securely verifies the provided phone number against the School's pre-existing records to authorize access to that specific student's portal.
---

2. Categories of Information Processed

The Application accesses and processes information split into two main classifications: Information Authorized by the School (Core Features) and Automatically Processed Technical Data.

A. School-Provided Student & Parent Records

The Application acts as a pipeline to securely display the following pre-existing educational records to authorized parents:

  • Student Academic Information: Classroom/section assignments and official academic report cards.
  • Attendance & Logistics: Daily or period-by-period attendance logs (e.g., Present, Tardy, Unexcused Absence).
  • Health and Safety: School clinic or infirmary visit logs.
  • School Resource Management: Library account status, borrowed books, and reservation logs.
  • Financial & Operational Status: School tuition fees, active contract structures, outstanding balances, and official payment receipts.
  • Communications: Direct school-to-parent messages, administrative announcements, and real-time operational notifications.

B. Automatically Processed Technical & Device Data

To ensure application stability, performance optimization, and secure session management, the Application may automatically process limited technical metadata:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address and device-specific network identifiers.
  • Mobile operating system name, version, and hardware specifications.
  • App diagnostics (e.g., crash logs, session durations, screen engagement, and UI interactions).
---

3. Purpose of Processing and Lawful Basis

We process information exclusively to fulfill contractual obligations between the Service Provider and the participating Schools, acting strictly as a Data Processor. The data is utilized to:

  • Authenticate authorized parents and prevent unauthorized access to sensitive student records.
  • Deliver real-time updates regarding student progress, school announcements, and emergencies.
  • Facilitate smooth transmission of administrative data, library notifications, and school fee tracking.
---

4. Third-Party Data Access & Disclosures

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any personal data or student records with third-party advertisers or data brokers. Data is shared only under the following tightly controlled circumstances:

  • With the Institutional School: Because the data originates from the School, all interactions, acknowledgments, or inputs made within the App are securely synchronized back to the School.
  • With Trusted Infrastructure Providers: We utilize trusted, enterprise-grade cloud hosting and push notification delivery services (such as Apple Push Notification service) that operate under strict confidentiality agreements. These providers have no independent right to use or access the data.
  • Legal Obligations: We may disclose information if required by applicable law, such as to comply with a valid legal process, subpoena, or government safety mandate.
---

5. Children’s Privacy & Parental Control

The Application inherently handles student data that may include children under the age of 16. However, because this data is strictly managed and provisioned by the School—and accessible only by verified parents or legal guardians—the Application complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and relevant regional educational privacy laws.

We do not knowingly solicit data directly from children, nor is the app designed for independent child usage without parental supervision.

---

6. Data Retention and Deletion

  • School Records: Academic, financial, clinical, and attendance records shown in the app are controlled entirely by the School's master retention policies. If a student withdraws or graduates, access permissions are revoked by the School.
  • Cached/Temporary Data: Device-side local caching used to accelerate app performance is safely cleared upon user log-out or uninstallation.
  • Account Disassociation: To completely wipe locally stored session files or request verification data removal, parents may contact us at info@deghost.com, or directly coordinate with their School's registrar.
---

7. Data Security Safeguards

We employ robust, industry-standard physical, electronic, and managerial safeguards to protect your data. This includes end-to-end encryption of data in transit (via HTTPS/TLS), secure API token authentications, and regular server security patching. In the highly unlikely event of a verified data breach, we maintain strict operational protocols to notify affected institutional clients and users in accordance with applicable legal timelines.

---

8. Your Rights & International Transfers

Depending on your jurisdiction, you or your School may retain rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of personal data. Because the Service Provider operates on behalf of international clients, data may be transferred to and maintained on cloud infrastructure located outside your home country. We utilize recognized legal transfer mechanisms, such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), to ensure equivalent safety standards globally.

Your continued use of the Application post-update indicates your acknowledgement of the amended terms.
